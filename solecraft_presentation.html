TRANSACTION

Cart & Checkout

Frictionless transition from design to purchase.

Session-Based Cart

Users can add custom designs to their cart without logging in. State is preserved via local storage and session persistence.

Design Snapshots

At checkout, the precise hex codes, materials, and size selections are compiled into a JSON payload and attached to the order.

Payment Gateways

Integrates simulated credit card payments and Cash on Delivery (COD), instantly generating an Order ID upon success.
